# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a constructive, technically rigorous community. Participants should be respectful, welcome questions, critique ideas with evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and make room for people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed on the GitHub repository. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban a participant when necessary. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as possible.

Maintainers are expected to explain moderation decisions when doing so does not compromise privacy or safety.
